What we collect, what we don’t, and who else is in the room.

The short version

You don’t sign up for anything here. The site sets no cookies, and it has no analytics or ads. Your saved cafes stay on your phone. If any of that changes, this page changes first.

What stays on your device

When you tap ♡ on a cafe, the site keeps that list in your browser’s own storage on your device. It is never sent to us, so we can’t see it. Clear this site’s data in your browser settings and it is gone.

Other services you meet while browsing

  • Google Fonts. The site’s typefaces load from Google. Your browser asks Google’s servers for them, so Google can see your IP address and browser details, under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles connection data such as your IP address to deliver pages and keep the site safe.
  • Maps and WhatsApp links. They only open when you tap them. After that, Google Maps or WhatsApp applies its own rules.

When you suggest a cafe

The suggest form doesn’t store anything on this site. It opens your WhatsApp or email app with a message ready, and you choose whether to send it. If you do, we receive that message along with your name, number or email address as your app shows it. We use it to read your suggestion and to reply if we need to. We don’t sell it.
