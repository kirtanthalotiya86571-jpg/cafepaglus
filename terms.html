What this site is

cafepaglus is honest cafe reviews for Ahmedabad. The scores and notes are our team’s opinions, written after our own visits.

Opinions, on a particular day

A review reflects what we found when we visited. Menus, prices, opening hours, parking, and pure veg or Jain options can change. Please check with the cafe before you make a trip for it.

How we work

We pay for our own meals. No sponsored reviews, no paid placements, no freebies for a score.

Using the site

  • It’s free to use and share. Send the link to your group chat.
  • Please don’t copy our reviews, scores or photos onto other sites without asking us first.
  • Please don’t try to break, overload or scrape the site.

Other people’s sites

We link to services like Google Maps and WhatsApp. They are not ours, and we are not responsible for what they do.

No guarantees

We work hard to be accurate, but the site is provided as it is. To the extent the law allows, we aren’t responsible for a disappointing meal, a closed door or a full car park.
